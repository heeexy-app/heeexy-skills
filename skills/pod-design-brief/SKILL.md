# Print-on-demand design brief

## 1. Clarify the idea

Niche and buyer (who wears or gifts it), occasion, the exact words on the design (if any), style (retro, groovy, watercolor, line art, distressed, kawaii, varsity), color limits, and the products it must work on.

## 2. Print specs per product

| Product | Canvas (px at 300 DPI) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Adult t-shirt front | 4500 × 5400 | transparent PNG; keep key art inside the center 85% |
| Youth / toddler shirt | 3300 × 3960 / 2400 × 2880 | simpler shapes, larger text |
| 11 oz mug wrap | 2700 × 1100 | keep text away from the handle area |
| 15 oz mug wrap | 2850 × 1200 | |
| 20 oz skinny tumbler | 2790 × 2460 | seamless left and right edges |
| Sticker | 1500 × 1500 or larger | add a 30–40 px white offset border |
| Tote bag | 3600 × 3600 | |

Always: transparent background, sRGB, 300 DPI, no stray semi-transparent pixels at the edges, text converted to the final spelling.

## 3. Write the AI image prompt

Structure: subject + action/pose → style → composition → color palette → text in quotes → technical terms.
Example: `cute retro sunshine mascot holding a coffee cup, 1970s groovy cartoon style, centered isolated composition, warm orange mustard and cream palette, the words "Rise and Shine" in bold rounded retro lettering above, clean vector-like edges, transparent background, t-shirt print design`.

Give three prompt variants (safe, bolder, minimalist) and one negative list (no background scene, no watermark, no misspelled text, no cropped elements).

## 4. Checklist before publishing

- Spelling of every word checked letter by letter.
- No trademarks, logos, celebrity names, sports teams, song lyrics or movie quotes.
- Readable at thumbnail size; lines thicker than about 2 px at print size.
- Edges clean on a dark and a light shirt mockup.
- File named with the listing keywords (`retro-teacher-sublimation-png.png`).
