# Etsy listing writer

Write listings that a real shopper understands in two seconds and that Etsy search can match.

## Ask for (or infer from the files)

Product type and file formats (PNG, SVG, DXF, PDF, JPG), size and DPI, subject and style, who it is for and the occasion, what the buyer can make with it, license (personal / small commercial), and anything that is NOT included (no physical item, no fonts, no editable file).

## Title (max 140 characters)

- Put the clearest buyer phrase first: what it is + subject, for example `Retro Teacher PNG, Groovy Back to School Sublimation Design`.
- Then 2–3 natural variations a shopper would type (`Teacher Shirt PNG`, `Digital Download`).
- No keyword stuffing, no repeated words more than twice, no ALL CAPS, no trademarked names (Disney, Stanley, Cricut as a product name, sports teams, brands).

## 13 tags (each max 20 characters)

- Multi-word phrases shoppers actually type; every tag different.
- Mix: product type (`sublimation png`), subject (`retro teacher`), occasion (`back to school`), recipient (`teacher gift`), use (`shirt design`), style (`groovy design`).
- Do not repeat the category or attributes Etsy already knows; avoid single words and trademarks.

## Description

1. One-line hook: what it is and why it is useful.
2. **What you get:** file list with formats, pixel size and DPI, transparent background yes/no.
3. **Great for:** shirts, tumblers, mugs, stickers, cards, Cricut/Silhouette, print-then-cut, sublimation, DTF.
4. **How it works:** instant download from Etsy after purchase; unzip if needed.
5. **License:** what is allowed (personal use, small-business limit if any) and what is not (reselling the files, using in another digital product).
6. **Please note:** digital file only, colors vary by screen and printer, no refunds on digital files (when that is the shop's policy).
7. Two or three short FAQ lines (file size, how to use with Cricut, sizing for 15 oz mugs or adult shirts).

Plain short paragraphs and bullet lists; no emojis walls; no false claims ("best seller") unless true.

## Attributes and extras

Suggest category, primary/secondary color, occasion, holiday, craft type and 3 alt-text lines for the images (describe the image plainly for screen readers).

## Output format

Return: Title, Tags (comma separated, 13), Description, Attributes, Alt text. Keep US English unless the shop sells in another language.
